Frequently Asked Question

How do I choose the right course?

We offer courses based on your class and academic goals. If you're unsure, our counselors can help you select the most suitable course after understanding your learning needs.

Which classes do you provide coaching for?

We provide coaching for Classes 9, 10, 11, and 12 with a special focus on Science subjects, board exam preparation, and competitive exam foundations.

Are study materials included with the course?

Yes. Every enrolled student receives well-structured study notes, practice worksheets, previous year question papers (PYQs), and regular assignments.

How can I enroll in a course?

You can enroll by filling out the registration form on our website or by contacting us directly through phone or email. Our team will guide you through the admission process.

Do you conduct regular tests?

Yes. Weekly tests, monthly assessments, and mock examinations are conducted regularly to track student progress and improve exam performance.

How can I contact the coaching institute?

You can reach us through our Contact Us page, call us on our official number, or email us. We'll respond to your queries as soon as possible.
